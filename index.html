FAQ

Is the rendering real?

Yes. ARPO renders with real FFmpeg — in your browser for instant edits, or on our cloud for heavier jobs. Nothing is faked.

Do I need editing skills?

No. You describe what you want in words; ARPO builds the edit plan and renders it.

What videos work best?

Talking-head, podcasts, vlogs and promos under a few minutes. Cloud render handles clips up to ~60s in beta.

Who owns my videos?

You do. Uploads are processed for your edit and never shared.

Can I automate ARPO with n8n?

Yes — render events (completed/failed) can fire webhooks to your n8n workflows.
